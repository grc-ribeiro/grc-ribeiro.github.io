Affordance and mapping is really similar. What are the main differences?

They’re quite different, actually. In a car, the window controls illustrate both affordance and mapping. A switch that can be pressed down and pulled up affords those actions, and its shape helps the driver recognize how to use it. Mapping is the relationship between each switch and the window it controls; the switches are arranged to correspond to the windows’ positions, so the driver can more easily tell which switch operates each window.
