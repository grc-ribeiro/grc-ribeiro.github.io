Report update Module 7

Tasks

On module 7, this is what is expected on your report:

Wireframes

Include the wireframes for keypath scenarios and some important validation scenarios for your product.

Actual content: No placeholder or filling.

Establish visual hierarchy: Remember proximity, alignment, visibility, etc.

No color palette: Only green or red if semantically necessary.

No typeface selection concerns: Use Helvetica, Franklin Gothic, or Roboto.

Placeholder for images: No final images, only rough sketches.

Check for inconsistency: Sizes, position, language, functionality, etc.

You can use any tool that you want, but I suggest Figma since your next step is to make it interactive.

Rubric

  • Scenario coverage

    20 points

    Includes wireframes for all keypath scenarios and appropriate validation scenarios. Screens and states clearly support the user’s goals.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Content and labeling

    20 points

    Uses realistic, product‑specific content rather than placeholder text. Controls, headings, information, and actions are clearly labeled.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Visual hierarchy and layout

    20 points

    Establishes a clear hierarchy through proximity, alignment, grouping, size, visibility, and spacing. Important content and actions are easy to find.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Consistency

    10 points

    Maintains consistent sizes, positions, terminology, controls, and expected functionality across screens.

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Wireframe fidelity and visual restraint

    10 points

    Uses a wireframe-level visual treatment: approved typeface, no color palette, and only semantic red or green when necessary. Images are represented with rough placeholders or sketches.

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Clarity and completeness

    10 points

    Wireframes are neat, legible, and sufficiently detailed to understand what users see, do, and experience next. The work is ready to be made interactive.

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Check with instructor

    10 points

    Did you check your screens and solutions with me before leaving the lab?

    • 10 pts Yes
    • 0 pts No

Submission

  1. Submit your PDF to Canvas using the name convention groupname-m7.pdf.

  2. Always submit the whole document as it is now. One submission per group is sufficient.

  3. Good typography will be always required and expected in this class.

  4. Please check Canvas for deadline.
