GRC 4900: Assignments

Report update Module 2

Every week, you will work on the same assignment, which is a report that will be updated with new content as the project evolves. Please, download the report template and follow the instructions for each week.

Tasks

On module 2, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Cover

    Name of the institution, name of the department, number, and name of the class; (tentative) name of the project; one-line description; name of the students (in alphabetical order by last name); date. Don’t edit on the page itself. Use variables.

  2. Table of contents

    The table of contents will be automatically generated if you continue to use paragraph styles, which you should anyway. Go to Layout > Update Table of Content in InDesign before export your file to PDF.

  3. Schedule

    Make a schedule for your project. (It’s, of course, based on the schedule for the class, but they’re not the same thing.)

  4. Abstract (tentative)

    Write one or two paragraphs about your product. (It’s about the product, not about your process.) Try to answer these questions: What does it do? How does it do it? Why is it different? Write in present tense and don’t write the questions that it’s answering, just include the answers for the questions in the paragraphs.

  5. Audience

    Explain who you are designing for. If your audience is too broad, try to narrow it, by explaining the context on what where you envision your product being used and the problems you anticipate solving. Keep in mind that audience doesn’t mean demographics. You want to include characteristics that are relevant to the project as users, such as their proficiency with the subject matter, current challenges, and the context in which they experience the problem. (A persona is not required at this stage.)

  6. Competitive landscape

    On a professional, “real life” experience, the competitive analysis can take several weeks and address issues such as business model, that are not a concern for our academic experience in this course. Furthermore, since your product is innovative, it may not have a direct competitor. It does have, however, a competitive landscape where it will live. Search for images of user interfaces in similar or related products. You may also include physical products that your target audience currently use to accomplish the same goals or perform similar tasks. Also, include images of physical and digital products that share the same domain (e.g. hospital, teaching, driving, sorting, rescuing, etc). Finally, you want to include helpful captions for all your images. (“AR glasses” is not that helpful; you need something like “Apple Vision Pro (2024) being used as an augmentative tool in medical surgery”.)

Rubric

  • Cover

    10 points

    Did you check if you have all the requirements for the cover?

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Table of contents

    20 points

    Did you update the TOC and it worked correctly? (If you didn’t use an automatic TOC, I guarantee you that it will show eventually.)

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Abstract

    20 points

    Did you explain what the product does? (Not what it would do or will do.) Does the abstract answer the three questions it was supposed to answer (without addressing the questions)?

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Audience

    20 points

    Did you make it clear who you are designing this product for? Did you include characteristics that are relevant to the product use rather than demographic data?

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Competitive landscape

    30 points

    Did you include enough examples for functionality, form factor, and visual language? Did you include captions that actually inform what one is looking at?

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  3. Good typography will be always required and expected in this class.
