Report update Module 3

Tasks

On module 3, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Research strategy

    Explain the objectives of the interview and how you are going to recruit users and other stakeholders.

  2. Research questions

    List all the questions that you plan on asking. Keep them broad enough to spark conversation but specific enough to get you the answers that you need.

Rubric

  • Research strategy

    30 points

    Make sure you explain the objectives of the interview and how you are going to recruit users and other stakeholders.

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Research questions

    70 points

    Do you have a list of questions to help you get the information that you need? Are they broad enough to spark conversation but specific enough to get you the answers that you need?

    • 70 pts Satisfactory
    • 50 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 25 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

If there are points missed from last week, they need to be addressed or they will be deducted again, but doubled.
