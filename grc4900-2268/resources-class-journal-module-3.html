GRC 490: Resources

Class journal, module 3

I am also taking User Experience Research this semester and its interesting to see how the two classes overlap in terms of content.

I had a meeting with HY and Christine this week, and we are confident that GRC 3000, GRC 4290, and GRC 4900 do overlap on important points and have their uniqueness to best serve the students.

I’m finding that the million-dollar answer this quarter is “It depends”.

This semester. But I know what you mean.

It is important to get user input, but they should not be designing or making final financial decisions.

Definitely not making the final decision.

Ethnographic interviews sound way more complicated than they actually are.

I know. They do.

It was a difficult balancing act trying to ask broad enough questions while also gathering the right amount of information we would need to move forward.

It is indeed a challenging task. But I gotta say that I was very satisfied with the results I saw in both labs this week. You all found creative questions to get your answers. I hope it goes as well as possible in the interviews.

When asking a “scale of 1–10” question, it is best to ask why the user answered that way to understand their thought process.

Exactly. That number won’t mean much to you (or to the user). But it can spark some ideas about their likes and dislikes.

When possible, interview users in the environment they would typically use the product being discussed.

It tends to help.

I learned today that it’s a fantastic idea to write down many ideas and narrow it down from there. My group struggled to come up with question ideas, but we wrote down too many (way too many) and then simplified it to a consistent number at around 10 questions for our interviews.

Yes. That’s how you do it.

Our group found it effective to write down all of the ideas we had, then talk about them as a group and edit/ narrow down.

That’s how you work in groups.

Do not interview GrC students or faculty as it may give biased answers due to their background.

Correct.

I’m looking forward to hearing what our interviewees will have to say in our interviews and how it can help inform our group’s decisions.

So am I! And that goes for all groups.
