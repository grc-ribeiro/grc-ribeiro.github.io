GRC 4900: Assignments

Report update Module 8

Tasks

On module 8, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Wireframes

    Choose three common scenarios for using your product. Focus on the actions people are most likely to perform. Update the wireframes with all the screens that you will need in your usability analysis test.

  2. Usability analysis tests

    Build an interactive wireframe prototype. Make sure your tester can click through the flows needed to complete all three tasks. Write your three tasks. Your prompts should describe a goal without revealing the steps or repeating the words used in your interface.

Rubric

  • Scenario coverage, content and labeling

    20 points

    Includes wireframes for all keypath scenarios and appropriate validation scenarios. Screens and states clearly support the user’s goals. Uses realistic, product‑specific content rather than placeholder text. Controls, headings, information, and actions are clearly labeled.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Visual hierarchy, layout, and consistency

    20 points

    Establishes a clear hierarchy through proximity, alignment, grouping, size, visibility, and spacing. Important content and actions are easy to find. Maintains consistent sizes, positions, terminology, controls, and expected functionality across screens.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Wireframe fidelity, visual restraint, clarity, and completeness

    10 points

    Uses a wireframe-level visual treatment: approved typeface, no color palette, and only semantic red or green when necessary. Images are represented with rough placeholders or sketches. Wireframes are neat, legible, and sufficiently detailed to understand what users see, do, and experience next.

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Usability test prompts

    30 points

    Prompts are clear and achievable through the interactive prototype. Prompts describe a goal without revealing the steps or repeating the words used in the interface.

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Interactive prototype

    20 points

    You demonstrated to the instructor in the lab that your tasks are all achievable through the interactive prototype before you left the lab.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory
