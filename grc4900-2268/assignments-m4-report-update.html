GRC 4900: Assignments

Report update Module 4

Tasks

On week 4, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Summary of the answers
    For each participant of the ethnographic interview, list the main findings. You may write in paragraphs or bullet points. (You don’t need to include the full transcript of each interview.)

  2. Persona
    Introduce your persona, with a name, age, and a picture. Describe, in a paragraph, information about their lives that are relevant to your project. Include a quote to summarize their main goal. One persona per project only.

  3. Goals
    List what your persona would accomplish with your product. Don’t list your goals for the project. It’s the persona’s goals.

Rubric

  • Summary of the answers

    20 points

    Did you check if you have all the requirements for the cover?

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Persona

    50 points

    Did you include a name, a picture, and a quote? Did you follow the lecture guidelines for good practice on personas? Did you include the characteristics that are relevant to the project? Remember to be precise, although not accurate.

    • 50 pts Satisfactory
    • 40 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 25 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Goals

    30 points

    Did you list the end goals as well as a life goal and an experience goal?

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

Submission

  1. Submit your PDF to Canvas using the name convention groupname-m4.pdf.

  2. Always submit the whole document as it is now. One submission per group is sufficient.

  3. Good typography will be always required and expected in this class.

  4. Please check Canvas for deadline.
