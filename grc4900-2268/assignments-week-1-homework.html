GRC 4900: Assignments

Homework (week 1)

I believe that coming up with an idea for the project is the biggest challenge in this course. That’s why we have this homework activity. It will be graded as if it was the report update for week 1.

In this course, you will work for 15 weeks on the same project. It can be a web service, a smartphone app, a kiosk interface, or anything that has a visual interaction interface for the final user to interact with. (You don’t need, however, to limit yourself to devices you already own or know.)

Think about emerging technology, even if it’s not feasible commercially. (Tele‑transportation is off‑limits, but wireless electricity is fair game.) Here are some ideas for emerging technology you can work with. You may, of course, combine more than one item from the list.

  1. Ultra fast wireless internet
    Think 100TB/s or more.

  2. Wireless electricity
    Not only at home or at work, but on the streets, everywhere.

  3. Self-driven cars
    What are the possibilities beyond checking social network apps while commuting?

  4. Wearable devices of all kinds
    Not only glasses or watches. Think about necklesses, rings, shoes, whatever…

  5. Smart home
    What if everything at your home was connected to the internet?

  6. Augmented reality
    How technology can give us the information that we need on each context?

Come up with 12 to 20 ideas. Think about different devices, scales, and kinds of product. Think about your own interests as well as other people’s (travel, sports, studies, music, camping, etc.)

Here are some ideas:

  1. An app that will play music according to your mood

  2. An cooking app that will give you recipe with what you got in your fridge or pantry

  3. Maps for finding hidden classrooms or empty study spots

  4. Tools to find classmates for group projects based on free time

  5. Clean calendars that sync homework tasks with class times

  6. Simple apps to log daily moods and find breathing exercises

  7. Water and break reminders during long study hours

  8. Fitness motivators

  9. Alerts for free food left over from campus events

  10. Safe ridesharing boards for students heading home on weekends

  11. Quick scanners to check which bin local trash belongs in

  12. Any kind of note taker or task organizers

You can cross off all the above ideas from your list.

Submit your actual ideas on Canvas (as plain text) before the lab.
