Report update Module 11

Tasks

On module 11, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Video script

    Write a script for a one-minute video based on your persona and scenario. The video should show how your persona uses your application to complete one meaningful task.

    Include a title, setting or context, the persona’s goal or problem, the relevant interface interactions, and any dialogue, narration, or on-screen text. Keep the story focused. A one-minute video usually includes about 130–150 spoken words.

  2. Storyboard

    Create a storyboard for the same one-minute video. Include approximately six to ten frames that show the sequence of the video from beginning to end.

    For each frame, include a sketch or layout of what the viewer sees, a brief description of the action, and any dialogue, narration, or on-screen text. Include notes about transitions, camera movement, or interface interactions where appropriate.

    Your storyboard drawings do not need to be polished illustrations. They should clearly communicate the story, sequence, and interface interactions you plan to show in the video.

Rubric

  • Video script

    40 points

    The script clearly establishes the persona, scenario, and user goal. It presents a focused, logical story that can reasonably fit in one minute and clearly explains the important interface interactions.

    • 40 pts Satisfactory
    • 30 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 20 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Storyboard

    40 points

    The storyboard includes an appropriate number of clear, organized frames. It translates the script into a logical visual sequence and communicates the relevant actions, interface interactions, dialogue, narration, and on-screen text.

    • 40 pts Satisfactory
    • 30 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 20 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Connection to persona and scenario

    10 points

    The script and storyboard clearly reflect the needs, goals, and context of the project’s persona and scenario.

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Check with instructor

    10 points

    Did you check your video script and storyboard with me before leaving the lab?

    • 10 pts Yes
    • 0 pts No
