Several objects that I appreciate here. First, I love a analog clock; I have several at home. I also like analog watches; I wore them since high school until 2018, when I bought my first Apple Watch. I miss them, but one thing that stops me from going back is that I know the time is always right on the Apple Watch.

Also, just like analog clocks, I like the paper calendar on my wall. And everybody should invest in a nice pair of scissors. I don’t use a planner, but I always have sticky notes in my bag, and I always carry a journal and two pens in my pocket. (I’ve never cared for mechanical pencils, though.) And one student mentioned that they use paper and pen at their job waiting tables. “We don't use the little handheld order-taking devices and I definitely don't just remember orders in my head.” I so appreciate it. As a patron of a restaurant, I don’t love when waiters use the electronic devices, but I hate it when they don’t write it down at all. It makes me anxious.

I never had a sewing machine, but I’ve always found them fascinating. And for some reason, I’ve always like thimbles; I think they’re fun.

Food thermometer and tweezers would not make my top list, but I sure appreciate them. Especially tweezers. Microwave, on the other hand, I use almost every day, but I don’t like it. I’m old enough to remember when we didn’t have them at home. It was certainly a nice convenience, but I’d rather not use it if I can avoid it. I reheat my leftovers in pot and pans instead. Or the air fryer. The lemon squeezer has always been a favorite of mine too. My parent had one in orange‑red that looked awesome with a very 1970s aesthetic.

I have a collection of physical maps and appreciate good signage, of course. They’re both wonderful examples of good visual communication design.

Several of you mentioned the water bottle. Funny to think that we didn’t have one when I was in college. Not sure what we did. Probably didn’t drink much water.

I find it interesting that nobody mentioned glasses or contacts.

And I’ll add my backpack as my object of choice. Carrying everything I need, hands free!

Thanks for participating!
