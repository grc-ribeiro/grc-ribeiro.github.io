GRC 4900: Assignments

Report update Module 9

Tasks

On module 9, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. High-definition layouts

    In the User interface design chapter, in the section Annotated layouts include some visual language options for some of the screens in your application. You don’t need more than three or four screens, but have some different layouts for each one of them. Use actual proposes for color palette, typeface selection, actual images, etc. (Not wireframes anymore.)

  2. Justify your design decisions

    Explain why the interaction works. In the Final product chapter of your report, write a short paragraph for the Aesthetic–usability effect, Signal-to-noise ratio, Legibility, Color, and Accessibility design principles. Write in present tense and avoid first person narrative. Don’t write “We chose…”, but “The product does…”. (Again, check with me before submitting your work. That’s why we have labs.)

Rubric

  • High-definition layouts

    30 points

    Includes three or four key screens with multiple layout options for each. Layouts are high‑definition rather than wireframes, using actual proposed color palettes, typefaces, imagery, and interface details.

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Visual language and hierarchy

    30 points

    Visual choices create a clear hierarchy, support the product’s intended personality and audience, and use color, type, images, spacing, and components consistently.

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Design-principle justification

    30 points

    Includes a short present‑tense paragraph for each principle: Aesthetic–usability effect, Signal‑to‑noise ratio, Legibility, Color, and Accessibility. Explanations accurately connect each principle to specific product decisions and avoid first-person narrative.

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Check with instructor

    10 points

    Did you check your layouts and justifications with me before leaving the lab?

    • 10 pts Yes
    • 0 pts No

Submission

  1. Submit your PDF to Canvas using the name convention groupname-m9.pdf.

  2. Always submit the whole document as it is now. One submission per group is sufficient.

  3. Good typography will be always required and expected in this class.

  4. Please check Canvas for deadline.
