Report update Module 12

Tasks

On module 12, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Icon inventory

    Start by looking at the screens, tasks, and interactions you have already designed. In the User interface design chapter, add a section for your icon system. Write a list of every icon the product needs. (There’s no way this list will be complete. In fact, one could say that’s never complete, since design is never finished. Do your best)

  2. Platform considerations

    Explain how the icon choices support the platform where the application will be used. Consider established platform conventions, familiar symbols, touch-target size, labels, and accessibility.

  3. Icon system

    Define the visual rules for the icons in your application. Show the icons you have used from the platform library (SF Symbols or Google Material). Add captions to explain your decision for weight, proportion, color, etc. (You probably want to choose at least five to ten icons here.)

  4. Custom icons

    Design the icons that are specific to your application. Do not use generic icons when a custom icon would better represent an important feature, category, or action in your product. Show the final icons at the sizes in which they will appear in the interface. Make sure they remain recognizable, clear, and visually consistent. If you believe you don’t need to “reinvent the wheel” explain what custom icons you may have considered, but ultimately chose to use one from the platform library. (You probably don’t need more than one or two icons.)
