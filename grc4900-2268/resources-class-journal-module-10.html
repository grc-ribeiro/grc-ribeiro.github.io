An app that has a beautiful UI on my phone is probably VSCO. It has a very artistic photo gallery and creative vibe to it. It has a very minimalistic black and white color palette, yet its collage-style layouts give it lots of character and uniqueness compared to other social medias. It feels authentic and different as your posts are arranged based on whatever size your image is, whereas apps like Instagram have a default post siz that all your posts have to stick to. VSCO feels like it is made for people to freely express themselves.

I haven’t opened VSCO in many years, but it has always been a beautiful app.

An app that I think has a beautiful interface is Overcast, which is a podcast app on iPhone. While I’m not much of a podcast listener myself, I have some family members that use this app, and it has a really clean, minimalistic interface. The primary colors it uses are white and orange, and I feel like the content is generally spaced out nicely that all the podcasts my parents listen to aren’t overwhelming to look at.

I am an enormous fan of pinterests UI. On top of this, it very seamlessly attaches ads into the layout which I think is very satisfying and not intrusive compared to other websites and UI examples. Lastly, it’s sidebar buttons are extremely intuitive, clear, and easy to use. It is a website which knows its limitations, doesn’t overwhelm the user with dozens of complicated and useless buttons. It knows it’s purpose, which is for finding fun art/designs, and serves that purpose, nothing more, nothing less.
