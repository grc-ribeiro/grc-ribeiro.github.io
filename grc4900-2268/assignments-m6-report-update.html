Report update Module 6

Tasks

On module 6, this is what is expected on your report:

  1. Interaction framework

    Sketch out the screens for your persona do accomplish the goals described in the scenario from last week. For each step, make it clear what the user sees, what the user does, and what happens next. Label important controls and information. Connect screens or states with arrows. Use color only if absolutely necessary. (Check with me before submitting your work.)

  2. Justify your design decisions

    Explain why the interaction works. Use the Consistency, Mental model, Performance load, and Feedback design principles for your justification.

Rubric

  • Alignment with persona and scenario

    20 points

    Does the framework clearly supports the persona’s goals and follows the scenario’s context, needs, and constraints?

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Screens

    30 points

    Did you include all key screens or states needed to complete the task. Does each screen clearly show the information the user needs at that moment?

    • 30 pts Satisfactory
    • 25 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 15 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Labels and legibility

    20 points

    Are important controls, information, and actions are clearly labeled? Are the sketches are neat and readable? Did you use color only if necessary to communicate meaning?

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Justification of design decisions

    20 points

    Did you explain why the interaction works? Did you based your justifications on the Consistency, Mental model, Performance load, and Feedback design principles?

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Check with instructor

    10 points

    Did you check your screens and solutions with me before leaving the lab?

    • 10 pts Yes
    • 0 pts No

Submission

  1. Submit your PDF to Canvas using the name convention groupname-m6.pdf.

  2. Always submit the whole document as it is now. One submission per group is sufficient.

  3. Good typography will be always required and expected in this class.

  4. Please check Canvas for deadline.
