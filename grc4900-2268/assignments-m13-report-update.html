Report update Module 13

Tasks

On module 13, update the Design principles analysis section of your report.

For each principle below, write a short paragraph explaining how the product applies it. Describe the product in the present tense and avoid first-person narrative. Do not write “We chose…”; write “The product…” or “The interface…”.

Expectation effect, Control, Affordance, Mapping, Feedback, Hick’s law, Serial position effect, Wayfinding, Recognition over recall, and Iconic representation.

You can delete Chunking and Highlighting from the principles. Also, I believe the Picture superiority effect is there twice.
