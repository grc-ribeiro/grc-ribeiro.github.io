GRC 4900: Resources

Class journal, module 7

I haven’t really designed too much in Figma, so it was kinda hard. I couldn’t quite figure out the auto-layout but my group partner knew how to so that was helpful. Maybe I should’ve watched the Figma videos from the class!

Maybe you should have.

It’s cool to see how layout and content evolved from initial sketches. I feel like us as designers learn more about the user experience as we reconsider initial sketches and assumptions we made.

That’s the goal!

Having a “back” arrow in the interface is misleading to the user, not the most efficient button to incorporate.

True. We’ll talk about it in class.

I haven’t really used Figma before, so it has been fun learning a new tool.

I noticed that some of you really enjoyed it. It’s a fun tool.

I need to learn how to make components and variations in Figma. That would help the design process go faster and smoother.

It does.

I really enjoyed working in Figma for the first time. I find it very user friendly and love the assets panel options. Some features make screens look really realistic.

It is a great tool. Very powerful and intuitive, which is a hard balance to find.

A major purpose of creating wireframes is to ensure that you are actually addressing the important functions clearly and efficiently without being distracted by aesthetics.

Exactly.

It was trickier than expected to write the microcopy for buttons and make decisions for what would be intuitive and familiar for users!

Most steps seem trickier than expected, right? Sometimes, it’s hard to do the easy.

Putting the wireframes together really encouraged me to think about our interface from the user’s perspective, and about how and when to communicate the information that they need clearly. My team found several areas from our original sketches that we needed to change to make them clearer. Shout out design principles!

The process is working. It’s almost like I know what I’m doing.

Wireframes should not use color or consider font / image choices yet since that can harm findings during usability testing.

Correct. Your users would focus on the wrong things.

It helped our group to gather inspiration from existing apps to see what works for users and what wouldn’t translate well to our product.

Please keep looking for inspiration from other apps. Don’t ignore the users’ mental models.

Our group used dribbble.com/tags/ui for lots of great examples of UI design in the real world!

Good idea and good resource. Thanks for sharing, Lucas!

Today’s quiz was the perfect amount of easy, actually. Don’t listen to the haters. 🙏

Sometimes those haters do have a good point, though.
