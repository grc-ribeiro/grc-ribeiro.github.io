Figma

As I have mentioned to you before, we’ll be using Figma to prototype our apps. If you haven’t yet, I suggest you to start by creating an account and then exploring the videos below.

You can work with Figma on your browser, or you can download the apps for Mac, Windows, iOS, iPad, and Android.

I have included each one of the videos below on Canvas as a one‑question, ungraded quiz for you to keep track. I hope it helps.
