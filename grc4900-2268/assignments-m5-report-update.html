Report update Module 5

Tasks

On module 5, this is what is expected on your report:

Scenario

Write a story where your persona interacts with your product. It’s not a story where someone buys the product. They already have it. It should be a typical use case, but it should also include a few interactions with the product. Use your sketches with (typed) captions. The sketches don’t need to be high‑skilled pieces of art, but make them neat in Photoshop. (At very least, use the Levels tool to make the background white.)

Rubric

  • Persona-centered scenario

    20 points

    The scenario clearly reflects the persona’s goals, needs, context, and likely behaviors.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Story

    40 points

    The story has a coherent beginning, middle, and end. The sequence of events is easy to follow. It depicts a believable, everyday situation in which the persona would use the product. The scenario focuses on using the product, not purchasing or acquiring it. The story includes a few specific, meaningful interactions with the product and shows how it responds to the persona’s actions.

    • 40 pts Satisfactory
    • 35 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 20 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Sketches

    20 points

    Sketches clearly illustrate important moments, screens, actions, or transitions in the story. Sketches are neat, legible, and cleanly prepared in Photoshop. Backgrounds are white, and distracting marks or shadows have been removed.

    • 20 pts Satisfactory
    • 15 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 10 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Captions

    10 points

    Every sketch includes concise typed captions that clarify the action or interface element shown.

    • 10 pts Satisfactory
    • 8 pts Mostly satisfactory
    • 5 pts Partially satisfactory
    • 0 pts Unsatisfactory

  • Check with instructor

    10 points

    Did you check your scenario with me before leaving the lab?

    • 10 pts Yes
    • 0 pts No

Submission

  1. Submit your PDF to Canvas using the name convention groupname-m5.pdf.

  2. Always submit the whole document as it is now. One submission per group is sufficient.

  3. Good typography will be always required and expected in this class.

  4. Please check Canvas for deadline.
