Tableau Public: Getting started

For the second half of GRC 3060, we will be working with Tableau Public, a free software for data visualization. Please follow the instructions below before the lab.

Instructions

  1. Create your account

    Go to public.tableau.com, click Sign In or Sign Up, and enter your name, email address, country, and role to register your free profile. Check your email to confirm and activate your new account.

  2. Download the installer

    If you want to use Tableau on your laptop, visit the Tableau Public download page, select your operating system (Windows, Mac Intel, or Mac Apple Silicon), fill out your details, and click Download the App.

  3. Install the software

    Open the downloaded setup file (.dmg for Mac or .exe for Windows), accept the license agreement, choose your installation preferences, and click Install.

  4. Sign in on desktop

    Launch Tableau Public on your computer, click on Open from Tableau Public and sign in with your newly created Tableau account credentials
